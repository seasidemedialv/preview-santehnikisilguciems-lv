Combining the bathroom and toilet - in a Soviet series flat

Combining the bathroom and toilet in a Soviet series flat in Iļģuciems means taking down the partition between the separate toilet and the small bathroom. For the plumbing, the most important thing is the riser in the boxed-in duct in the toilet: it is the building’s common property, it must stay accessible, and work on it is usually agreed with the building manager. Ask your building manager before the work starts which documents the wall demolition itself needs.

What to check in the flat before drawing a plan

  1. Open the toilet duct Behind the hatch there are usually the cold and hot water risers, the soil stack, the flat’s valves and the meters. Photograph them. In the new plan the valves and meters must be reachable, so that the water can be shut off and the readings taken.
  2. Identify the riser’s material Grey, rough and heavy is cast iron, smooth and light-coloured is plastic. If the cast iron is old and has rust at the joints, it is worth replacing its section on your floor during the renovation, while the walls are still open.
  3. Find the heated towel rail In many series bathrooms the heated towel rail is on the hot water riser. If you want to move it to another wall, it is no longer a simple replacement, and you need the building manager’s approval.
  4. See whether there is a radiator on the partition A heating radiator in an apartment building cannot simply be moved. It has its own procedure, and the work is done outside the heating season.
  5. Measure the distance from the new fixture position to the riser If the toilet or shower is moved further from the riser, the pipe needs a longer fall. Every metre needs more height, and the floor may rise.

The riser in the toilet duct stays with the whole building

When rooms are combined, people often want to demolish the toilet duct or tile it shut. But the riser serves all the flats one above another, and it may need to be reached in an emergency or a building repair. So leave a hatch or a removable panel opposite the valves, the meters and the riser joints.

The Apartment Ownership Law obliges a flat owner to treat the parts in common property with care. Under Article 13 of this law, a flat owner pays the costs of replacing the building’s common services (for example the risers) in line with their share of the common property, following the flat owners' association’s decision. How this applies to your renovation is usually set by the building’s decisions; ask your building manager. Checked 16.11.2026.

Replacing a riser section needs the building manager’s permission and the water shut off for the whole riser. With a soil stack, the upstairs neighbours must not run water meanwhile, so the work is announced in advance.

The towel rail and the radiator need their own approval

In buildings of Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga’s municipal building manager), a heated towel rail can be replaced like for like in the same place on application (without reducing the diameter and promising to fix leaks within three days). Moving it or changing its connection needs an approved project, like a radiator. Under AS “Rīgas siltums” rules, a radiator or heated towel rail in an apartment building is replaced only to technical documentation, with a heat loss calculation, prepared by a heat supply designer holding a certificate and agreed with the building manager. Rīgas siltums gives roughly 1 May–1 October as a general window to shut off and drain a riser; the dates and permission come from the building manager (e.g. until 31 August in Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks buildings). Checked 16.11.2026.

Other building managers may have a different procedure, so ask yours. In practice this means: if you plan the combining for winter, leave room for the towel rail and radiator work for later, or plan the whole renovation for the warm season.

A toilet further from the riser needs a fall

The greatest freedom that combining gives is the chance to place the fixtures differently. But a drain works only with a fall, and that limits how far the toilet can be moved.

Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 on drains in the new layout (checked 16.11.2026). A toilet needs an outlet of 100 mm diameter; for a bath, shower and washing machine 40 mm is enough, for a hand basin 32 mm. If the fall is not calculated in a project, the thick toilet pipe (75–100 mm) falls 2 cm per metre, pipes up to 50 mm fall 3 cm per metre. In the middle of one run, between two connections, the fall must be kept the same.

Example: if the toilet’s 100 mm outlet is 2 m from the riser, the pipe needs a 4 cm fall, and the toilet sits higher or the floor is raised. If there is no room for the fall, the toilet stays by the riser and other fixtures are moved.

Questions residents ask

Can the toilet duct be tiled shut completely?

The valves, meters and riser joints must stay accessible, so a hatch or a removable panel is usually left. What exactly your building’s rules require, ask your building manager.

Who pays if a riser section has to be replaced during the renovation?

Article 13 of the Apartment Ownership Law ties the cost of replacing common services to the share of common property and the flat owners' association’s decision. If the section is replaced on a flat owner’s initiative, the procedure is usually set by the building manager, unless the building’s documents say otherwise. Ask your building manager before the work.

Do the neighbours have to know about riser work?

Yes, because the riser is shut off for all the flats on it. The shut-off and the notice are usually organised by the building manager.
