Heating and radiators in Iļģuciems - who owns the heating pipe

Heating and radiators in Iļģuciems and the surrounding area start with one question: does the radiator heat from the building’s riser or from your own boiler?

In a block of flats, a radiator that works only together with the building’s system is by law usually part of the common property, so it is shut off and replaced with the building manager informed. In a private house the whole circuit belongs to you, and you choose when the work is done.

A series block’s riser against a private house’s boiler circuit

Iļģuciems has mostly series 316/318 silicate-brick blocks and series 464 five-storey panel blocks, and also the nine-storey series 602. In such a block a radiator is one section in a long system:

  • Under Article 4 of the Apartment Ownership Law, a radiator in a flat usually counts as the building’s common property if it heats only from the building’s shared services.
  • Under Article 3, the separate property is the utility lines up to the common-property risers, so the boundary is usually at the riser; unless the building’s documents say otherwise, ask your building manager.
  • Heat for the buildings of Iļģuciems and Grīva comes from the networks of the “Imanta” and “Zasulauks” heating stations of AS “Rīgas siltums” (Riga’s district heating company); the “Imanta” station is at Kurzemes prospekts 17.
  • The building’s heat substation and heating system are checked once a year at building level, not flat by flat.

In a private house in the detached-house streets of Iļģuciems, in Kleisti, Spilve or Voleri, there is no building manager:

  • The boiler, pump, expansion vessel, pipes and radiators are one closed circuit, and it belongs to the homeowner.
  • AS “Rīgas siltums” states that the procedure for replacing radiators in a private house is set by its owner.
  • Water can be drained from just the section needed, and no neighbour is left without heat.
  • The boiler’s internal parts are repaired by the heat source’s service; the plumber’s work is the pipes, radiators, pump and vessel.

Heating system repair while the riser is full

From autumn to spring the riser is hot, and it is not drained for one flat. In this period the jobs done are those that need no water drained: replacing a thermostatic head, replacing a valve stem seal on those models where the manufacturer allows it, tightening a nut, and bleeding air. What to start with for a specific fault is written on the page about radiator repair.

If it leaks so much that a container does not help, the riser is shut off by the building manager’s emergency service. An empty riser means cold radiators on every floor on it. That is why, for a planned radiator replacement, the date and permission come from the building manager; AS “Rīgas siltums” generally mentions a period of roughly 1 May to 1 October. More on the page about radiator replacement.

Installing a heating system in a private house

In a private house a plumber can build a new pipe manifold, add a radiator for a new room or rebuild the old circuit, connecting it to the existing heat source. The boiler or heat pump itself is chosen and connected as a separate job.

Before a new section is put into use, it is filled, the air is bled and all the joints are inspected in the hot system. If the water in the old circuit is dark, the new section is added after heating system flushing, so that sludge does not get into the new radiators.

Pump humming, pressure dropping: the parts of a private house’s circuit

The circulation pump drives water from the boiler through the radiators. If it hums, overheats or stands still, the air, the power supply and the rotor are checked. To replace a heating circulation pump, the valves on both sides of the pump are closed or the water is drained from the circuit, the new pump is fitted and the circuit is bled.

When the pressure in a heating system drops, water is escaping through a joint, the boiler’s pressure relief valve or the expansion vessel. If the air cushion in the vessel has lost pressure, the reading jumps up in a hot circuit and falls in a cold one. Replacing or recharging the expansion vessel is decided after inspecting the vessel; topping up the system without finding the cause means everything repeats a week later.

Jobs not to do yourself at a radiator

  • Do not close the riser valves in the cellar: the heat disappears for every flat on that riser.
  • Do not remove or move a radiator on the building’s riser without the building manager’s agreement, because it is usually part of the common property.
  • Do not box in the riser and its valves: Article 10 of the Apartment Ownership Law obliges you to let the building manager’s specialists do the work in your flat that the building’s services need.
  • Do not leave a dripping joint until spring: under Article 14 of the same law, a flat owner is liable for damage caused to others, including the flat below.

Questions residents ask

Is the radiator in my flat my property or the whole building’s?

Usually the building’s: the Apartment Ownership Law counts as common property the heating elements whose operation depends on the building’s services. Unless the building’s documents or the association’s decision say otherwise, this applies to a radiator on a riser too. For an exact answer about your building, ask your building manager.

Where does the heat for Iļģuciems blocks of flats come from?

The residential areas of Iļģuciems and Grīva are connected to the networks of the “Imanta” and “Zasulauks” heating stations of AS “Rīgas siltums”. The Bolderāja network is supplied by the boiler house at Gobas iela 33a, and in Daugavgrīva the heating station is at Lēpju iela 4.

Who checks the building’s heating system before the season?

Item 23.3 of Cabinet Regulation No. 876 provides that the heat user checks the heat substation and the heating and hot water systems once a year, after agreeing with the supplier; in Riga this is booked with AS “Rīgas siltums”. In a block of flats it is organised at building level, usually through the building manager.

Can heating work in a private house also be done in January?

It can, because the decision is yours and no neighbours have to be shut off. In winter a house without heating cools quickly, so a bigger job is split into stages: one part of the circuit is closed off, and the other radiators keep heating.
