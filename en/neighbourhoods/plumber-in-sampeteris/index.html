Houses in Šampēteris: what is connected to what

  • Detached houses with gardens. Nature and planted areas take up only 1.8% or 2.4 ha, so the greenery comes from the gardens around detached houses. The pipes from the ownership boundary to the house, and inside the house, belong to the owner.
  • Houses on district heating. The Šampēteris district is named among the areas that have no hot water on the day the Imanta and Zasulauks heat networks are tested. There a water heater is useful as a backup for a flat or house.
  • Streets with old tree avenues. Many Šampēteris streets are lined with old tree avenues. For a yard sewer line this can mean roots close to the pipe.

Šampēteris borders Imanta, Zasulauks, Pleskodāle and Zolitūde. The main links are Lielirbes iela, Irlavas iela, Šampētera iela, Volguntes iela and Kalnciema iela, as well as the Jūrmala railway line with Zasulauks station.

The city sewer in Šampēteris is new

Rīgas ūdens states that in Šampēteris new sewer networks have been built and centralised sewerage provided over the last 15 years. For a detached house this can mean that the connection to the street is relatively new, while inside the house the old pipes remain from the time when the waste water went elsewhere.

These two parts meet at the house outlet, and that is where the questions arise. Before any work in the yard, it helps to know three things.

  1. Where the ownership boundary is Rīgas ūdens explains that the boundary usually runs along the outer wall of the meter chamber. Where there is no chamber, it is set by the plot boundary or the street’s red line. Everything that leads from it to the house is your responsibility.
  2. What slope the outlet has From the cellar outwards, Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 requires the pipe to fall at least 20 ‰, that is 2 cm in every metre. The outlet is no narrower than the widest riser that flows into it.
  3. How far the first chamber is If the outlet is 100 mm, then under LBN 221-15 the distance from the last riser or cleanout to the axis of the first chamber may be no more than 12 m. If the distance is greater, an extra cleanout or chamber is needed (this is assessed on site).

Tree avenues and the yard sewer line

Old tree avenues along Šampēteris streets are a feature of the neighbourhood, but for a yard sewer line they mean roots. Roots look for moisture and push into the joints of old pipes, and the line starts to run more slowly. The first sign is usually water that stands for a long time in the shower or the first chamber after a bath or the washing machine.

Mechanical cleaning with a rotating cable cuts the roots and breaks up fatty deposits. If grease and sludge are then left on the pipe walls, the line is flushed with a high-pressure water jet. In both cases there must be access to the inspection chamber or cleanout.

In a green area the inspection chamber cover should be 20–70 mm above the ground. If the chamber in the garden has become overgrown with lawn or buried, find it and uncover it before the plumber’s visit, otherwise the job starts with searching.

A cellar lower than the street chamber

In a Cohesion Fund project, Rīgas ūdens built the Lielirbes iela and Kalnciema iela sewer collector on the stretch from Dreiliņu iela to Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve and extended the networks in Šampēteris. A detached house is now part of a large system in which the water level in the street network can rise, for example in heavy rain or because of a blockage.

That is why LBN 221-15 sets a rule for cellars. If a fixture there, such as a shower or washing machine, stands lower than the yard chamber cover, it must be connected through its own separate outlet, with protection against water from the street network flowing back into the cellar. In practice this role is filled by a backwater valve. If the appliance is below street level and the valve is not enough, the waste water is pumped up above that level.

Questions residents ask

Who repairs the pipe between the street and my house in Šampēteris?

The Law on Water Management Services makes the service provider maintain the network up to the ownership boundary. The owner is responsible for the section from the boundary to the house and for everything inside the house, unless your contract with Rīgas ūdens says otherwise.

Can roof rainwater be drained through the same backwater valve in the cellar?

No. The manufacturer of the electronic backwater valve, HL Hutterer & Lechner, forbids draining rainwater through the valve. The same manufacturer advises that the rooms below the backflow level have few users, and that the house has another toilet above that level which can be used while the valve is closed.

There is water standing in the yard chamber, but everything is draining in the house. Where is the blockage?

If water stands in the chamber, the blockage is usually further downstream: between this chamber and the next one or the city network. If the chamber is empty but water will not drain inside the house, the fault lies in the stretch from the house to the chamber. In the form, write what you see in the chamber.

Why does a shower in the cellar need a backwater valve after a conversion?

A cellar shower is usually lower than the nearest chamber cover, and for such a fixture LBN 221-15 requires its own outlet and protection against backflow from the street network. Without it, water from the street network can come back up through the shower drain.
