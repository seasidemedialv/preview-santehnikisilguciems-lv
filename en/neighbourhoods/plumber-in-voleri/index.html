Here most of the work is outside the rooms: the pipe from the meter chamber to the wall, a cellar drain that lies lower than the street inspection chamber, and the days when the water in the house is shut off. From the centre of Iļģuciems to Voleri it is about 5.2 km (our map reading), and Iļģuciems does not border Voleri.

Voleri: streets, port, nature reserve and ditch

  • Houses: in 2025, 211 people lived in Voleri over 531.5 ha, so there are few residential buildings.
  • Streets: the main one is Daugavgrīvas iela, and inside the neighbourhood Zilā iela and Voleru iela.
  • Port: two production sites since the 1960s; they are company premises, not household plumbing.
  • Krēmeri nature reserve: created in 1992 where sludge and sand were dumped on a meadow in 1976–1986.
  • Beķera ditch: separates Voleri from Beķermuiža, is gradually becoming overgrown, and its upper end has been filled in.

The public tap at Voleru iela 65 works in winter too

By Voleru iela 65 there is one of four Rīgas ūdens public taps that work in winter too. It is useful when the house’s supply is shut off for longer during repairs, for example when replacing the incoming supply pipe or an old valve at the meter: you can take drinking water there.

If the house receives water from the Rīgas ūdens network, the boundary is usually the outer edge of the meter chamber wall, and the pipe from the chamber to the house is the owner’s responsibility.

Such a pipe is replaced in one visit: dig it out, lay the new one and connect both ends. In the form write the distance from the chamber to the house in metres and the material of the old pipe, if you know it.

The highest point is 9 m, and in the west it is already meadow flatland

The highest point in Voleri is in the north-west of the neighbourhood, 9 m above sea level. The western part beyond the Beķera ditch already belongs to the marshy plain of Spilves pļavas.

For a house on lower ground, it is worth comparing the cellar fixtures with the cover of the nearest inspection chamber. Clause 259 of LBN 221-15 covers exactly this case. If a cellar sink, shower or floor drain is lower than the cover of the nearest chamber, they are connected to the sewer separately, with their own outlet and a device against water flowing back from the outer network. In practice this device is a backwater valve.

The Rīgas ūdens emergency overflow chamber in Voleri is automated and coordinated with the pumping station on Ilzenes iela and the Daugavgrīva wastewater treatment plant, so that less untreated waste water reaches the Daugava in heavy rain. That is the work of the city network. On the house side, the backwater valve closes if the water in the street sewer rises above the backflow level, which is usually taken as street level.

Questions residents ask

Which neighbourhoods does Voleri border?

By land Voleri borders only Bolderāja and Spilve, and across the Daugava Kundziņsala and Vecmīlgrāvis. In the form the street and house number are enough; if the house is by the Beķera ditch or the Krēmeri reserve, write that too.

How do I tell whether a cellar sink needs a backwater valve?

Compare the height of the sink or floor drain with the cover of the nearest inspection chamber in the yard. If the fixture is lower, building code LBN 221-15 requires its own outlet and a backflow device for it. A photo from the cellar and from the chamber helps the plumber assess this already on the call.

Is a backwater valve also suitable for a toilet in the cellar?

Not every one. The manufacturer KESSEL states that type 0, 1 and 2 valves are intended only for waste water without faecal matter, for example for a sink or shower. A toilet in the cellar needs a type 3 valve with an electrically controlled flap (EN 13564) or a small pumping unit that lifts the waste water higher.

How often should a cellar backwater valve be checked?

The manufacturer KESSEL states that the house owner checks it once a month: close and open the emergency flap a few times. A specialist services the valve at least every six months (EN 13564). If the flap moves stiffly or does not close fully, write about it in the form.
