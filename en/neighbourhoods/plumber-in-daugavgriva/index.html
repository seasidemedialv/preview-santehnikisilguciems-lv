The heat plant on Lēpju iela and the radiator replacement procedure

The AS “Rīgas siltums” heat plant “Daugavgrīva” is at Lēpju iela 4, Daugavgrīva. Rīgas siltums describes the general radiator replacement procedure, but the dates and the permission in your building are set by the building manager.

  1. Design A heating designer prepares technical documentation with a heat loss calculation, so that the new radiator suits the room and the riser.
  2. Approval The documentation is approved with the building’s manager. Without it, the riser is not shut off.
  3. Work window Rīgas siltums gives the warm months as a general window, about 1 May to 1 October. The exact day is set by the building manager, who shuts off and drains the riser.
  4. Replacement Once the riser is empty, the old radiator comes off and the new one is hooked up to the pipes already there; then the riser is refilled and the connections are checked.

So in winter you start with the paperwork: the design and the approval can be prepared then, so that the work itself takes place when the heating season is over. Rīgas siltums applies the same procedure to heated towel rails.

Flotes iela is the only road to the island

Daugavgrīva’s only link with the rest of Riga is Flotes iela across the Buļļupe, and inside the neighbourhood the main streets are Flotes iela, Parādes iela and Dzintara iela. A part that was not brought along means one more trip across the bridge, so it is worth writing the measurements in the form. If it is not known what exactly is broken, a photo and a short description are enough, and the plumber asks the rest on the call.

  • Radiator: cast-iron sections or a steel panel, the number of sections or the length, the distance between the supply pipes.
  • Heated towel rail: the centre-to-centre distance between the supply pipes, and whether it is on the hot water or the heating pipe.
  • Mixer tap or cistern: a photo from the front and the model, if visible.
  • Floor: whether a heavy part has to be carried up the stairs.

Questions residents ask

Can a radiator be replaced in January?

The AS “Rīgas siltums” procedure provides for radiator replacement outside the heating season, about May to September, as the riser has to be shut off and drained then. The exact period in your building is set by the building manager. In winter the design and the approval can be prepared. If a radiator leaks in winter, first report it to the building manager, because the manager shuts off the riser.

Can a heated towel rail be replaced with a new one without a design?

If the building is managed by Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks, a towel rail can be replaced like for like in the same place with an application, without reducing the diameter and undertaking to fix leaks within three days. Relocating it or changing the connection needs an approved design, as for a radiator. With other building managers, ask for their procedure.

A radiator on the top floor is warm only at the bottom. Does it need replacing?

Usually not. If a radiator is cooler at the top than at the bottom, it most likely has air in it, and it is bled through the bleed valve. Replacement is needed if the radiator leaks, is rusted or still does not heat after bleeding.

Why must water be run from the taps after a holiday?

Rīgas siltums advises running rarely used taps and showers for a few minutes, weekly at the least. After a longer absence, run the hot and cold water in every tap and shower before using it.
