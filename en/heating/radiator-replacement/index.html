Radiator replacement - the riser, the valve and the design

Replacing a radiator in an Iļģuciems series block is a job on the whole heating riser: under the procedure of AS “Rīgas siltums” (Riga’s district heating company), the riser is shut off and drained.

A like-for-like replacement or a new design

A consultation on the LV portāls (Latvia’s official legal information portal) states that replacing a radiator in a block of flats usually needs the agreement of the building manager and the flat owners' association, as well as a technical design. The exception is a like-for-like replacement.

AS “Rīgas siltums” explains that the design is prepared by a heating-system designer and agreed with the building’s manager. Ask your building manager whether your replacement counts as like-for-like: the agreement is given by the building manager and the flat owners' association. Ask before buying the radiator, because the answer decides whether a designer is needed.

Work at the wall and work on paper

  • Included: disconnecting and removing the old radiator.
  • Included: hanging the new radiator in the same place and connecting it to the existing supply pipes.
  • Included: checking the affected joints after the riser is refilled.
  • Not included: the designer’s calculation and documentation.
  • Not included: the building manager’s work on the riser, which is charged under the building manager’s procedure.
  • Not included: the radiator, valves and brackets; new supply pipes to another wall are assessed after inspection.

Riser empty, radiator new, riser full again

  1. A date from the building manager The building manager sets the day when the heating riser is shut off and drained.
  2. Removing the old radiator The radiator is disconnected from the supply pipes and lifted off its brackets.
  3. Connecting the new radiator The new radiator is hung and connected to the existing supply pipes; on a one-pipe riser the valve gets the setting the manufacturer requires.
  4. Refilling the riser The riser is reconnected and refilled with heating water under the building manager’s procedure.
  5. Checking the joints After refilling, the joints affected by the work are checked to see that they are tight.

The valve for the new radiator on a one-pipe riser

For a Danfoss RA-N radiator valve, the presetting can be chosen from 1 to 7, but at the factory it is set to N, fully open. The manufacturer states that in one-pipe systems exactly N must be used. A lower number restricts the flow through the radiator.

Danfoss also gives limits for these valves: working pressure up to 10 bar, temperature up to 120 °C. It helps to enter the valve model in the form together with the radiator details.

Why a summer replacement does not affect the neighbours above and below

AS “Rīgas siltums” states that in the buildings it serves, the preparation of the systems for the season is finished in September. So the most suitable time to replace a radiator is summer, up to the start of the heating season. Later, the heating has to be shut off in the flats above and below yours as well.

Dzirciems and Imanta also have series 104 blocks of 12 storeys and higher. Many flats sit on one riser there: if the replacement happens after the heating season has started, every flat on that riser is without heat while it is empty.

The specific date and permission come from the building manager; for example, Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga’s municipal building manager) allows radiators to be replaced in its blocks until 31 August. Elsewhere, ask your own building manager.

AS “Rīgas siltums”: the design, draining and refilling the riser, preparing for the season in September. Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks: a deadline of 31 August in its own blocks. LV portāls: the like-for-like exception. Checked 17.11.2026.

Radiator measurements and building details for the form

  • How many radiators you are replacing and whether they are on one riser.
  • The length and height of the old radiator in centimetres, with supply pipes from below or from the side.
  • Whether the radiator already has a bypass, and the marking on the valve body.
  • The building’s series or address, the floor and the building manager.
  • Whether the building manager has already given a date and whether they require a design.
  • Photos: the whole radiator and both supply pipes.

Installing radiators in a private house

In a private house the procedure for replacing radiators is set by the homeowner, AS “Rīgas siltums” states. If the circuit is not a one-pipe one, a Danfoss RA-N valve can be set to a step from 1 to 7 and so share the heat between the radiators. After the work the circuit is refilled and bled; see bleeding radiators.

A radiator for a new room with a new supply pipe, and replacing heating pipes, are a separate job, assessed after inspection.

The heated towel rail in the bathroom

In a series block bathroom, the rail most often depends on the hot water riser, not on the heating. For replacing it and the electric option, see heated towel rail replacement.

Two radiators in a series 104 block: the total

Example: in a flat in a 12-storey series 104 block in Dzirciems, two radiators are replaced on the existing supply pipes on the day the building manager has named. 2 × from €75 = at least €150 for labour. The radiators, valves, the designer’s work and the building manager’s work are not in this total.

Questions residents ask

What setting should the new valve on the building’s riser have?

For Danfoss RA-N valves in one-pipe systems, the manufacturer requires the setting N, fully open. Whether your riser is a one-pipe one is told by the building manager or the designer.

When does a radiator replacement count as like-for-like?

In the LV portāls consultation, a like-for-like replacement is named as an exception where a technical design is usually not needed. Whether your new radiator fits this exception is decided by the building manager. Send them the details of the old and the new radiator and ask before buying.

What happens to the riser after the radiator is replaced?

Under the procedure of AS “Rīgas siltums”, the riser is reconnected after the work, refilled with heating water and the joints affected by the repair are checked for tightness. Reconnecting and refilling the riser follows the building manager’s procedure and is not in the price from €75.
