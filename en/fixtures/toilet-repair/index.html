Toilet repair - when the cistern leaks silently

Toilet repair on Riga’s west side most often starts with a leak you cannot hear: the cistern lets water into the pan in a thin trickle, and only a dye test or the meter shows it.

In a repair the toilet stays where it is, and one cistern part or a seal is replaced. Replacing one part of the mechanism is from €35, the part extra.

One part for one price

The price from €35 includes one of these jobs:

  • replacing the fill valve, if the cistern does not fill or the water runs into the overflow;
  • replacing the flush valve, if the water keeps running into the pan after flushing;
  • replacing the button, if it sticks or does not flush.

Not included:

  • the part itself: a valve, a button, a seal;
  • another mechanism part in the same cistern: on the call you talk through the price;
  • the drain pipe from the toilet to the riser: from €30 per connection point;
  • a new toilet, if the ceramic is cracked: that is a different job, from €50.

Look for the leak from the cistern downwards

  1. Dye test in the cistern WaterSense, a programme of the US Environmental Protection Agency, advises putting food colouring into the cistern. If after 10 minutes the colour appears in the pan, the cistern leaks.
  2. Meter test Write down the cold water meter reading, use no water for two hours and look again. If the reading has changed, there is a leak somewhere (a WaterSense recommendation).
  3. Water level against the overflow pipe If water runs into the overflow tube, the fill valve does not shut in time. For one model Ideal Standard states that the critical level mark of the fill valve must be at least 25 mm above the top edge of the overflow pipe.
  4. Fill valve filter and membrane If the cistern fills slowly or hisses, a blocked filter may be the cause. In servicing, the manufacturer Wirquin advises taking out and washing the membrane and the filter, and Ideal Standard also advises cleaning the filter regularly.
  5. Base and socket Dampness or a smell at the base of the toilet points to the connection to the drains. The manufacturer Oatey names one reason: the toilet flange is set too low, and the seal cannot press tightly.

Tablets in the cistern shorten the life of the valves

Ideal Standard warns against putting chlorine or chemical products in the cistern, because they damage the cistern parts and can cause flooding. If a cleaning tablet sits in the cistern and the valve then starts to leak, after the repair the tablet is not put back, and the toilet is cleaned from the outside.

Facts about the cistern, so the right part is brought

  • the dye test result and the meter readings with the times;
  • the manufacturer on the cistern lid, button or button panel;
  • one or two buttons, the supply from the side or from below;
  • a toilet with the cistern on it or a cistern built into the wall;
  • a photo with the lid removed, showing both valves;
  • the floor, and whether you notice a smell at the toilet.

Concealed cistern repair behind the button panel

The valves of a built-in cistern are usually reached through an opening behind the button panel, and the wall does not have to be broken. The parts for such cisterns belong to a specific manufacturer, so the name on the panel is the most important fact in the form. If the panel is tiled over or glued, the repair starts with freeing it, and you talk this through on the call.

Under your toilet there is usually the neighbour’s toilet

In five-storey series blocks the toilets are usually one above another, so a leak at the base of the toilet usually ends up in the neighbour’s toilet or bathroom. The current standard LBN 200-21 also provides for sanitary rooms in multi-flat buildings to be placed above similar rooms. The Apartment Ownership Law says that a flat owner is responsible for damage caused to others.

If the ceramic has a crack or parts for the old cistern can no longer be found, a repair only postpones the problem. Then see toilet installation. If the water comes from the wall or from the riser duct, not from the toilet, start with the page water leak.

Repair price with an example

Example: replacing a fill valve and one more hour for small jobs at the same visit, for example tightening a leaking tap connection: €35 + €25 = at least €60 labour, parts extra. Questions residents ask

The meter did not move in two hours, but the colour appears in the pan. How can that be?

A small leak can go unrecorded. A meter expert in an LSM article states that a class C meter records water accurately from 15 l/⁠h, while older class A and B meters only from 60 and 30 l/⁠h. The dye test shows such a leak earlier.

May blue cleaning tablets go into the cistern after the repair?

Ideal Standard warns against putting chlorine or chemical products in the cistern, because they damage the parts. It is safer to clean the toilet from the outside and leave only water in the cistern.

The cistern no longer leaks, but there is still a smell at the base of the toilet. Why?

A smell at the base usually comes from the connection to the drains, not from the cistern. The causes and checks are described in the advice on sewer smell in a flat.
