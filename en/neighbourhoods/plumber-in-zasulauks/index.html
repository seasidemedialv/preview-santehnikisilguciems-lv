Test day, when there is no hot water at the tap

Rīgas siltums carries out a hydraulic test on the Zasulauks and Imanta heat substation networks, and during it there is no hot water in the Zasulauks residential area. The tests take place in summer by zone and last one day. The evening before, the heat carrier is cooled to +45 degrees, and if no faults are found, hot water is restored in the evening of the same day.

For one day many manage with a pan on the hob. If there are small children in the flat, or if the hot water break runs on because of the building’s own repair, the answer is an electric water heater in the bathroom or kitchen. It hangs on a solid wall by the existing supplies, and on the cold water inlet it must have a safety valve with no shut-off valve between the valve and the water heater.

Rīgas siltums informs building managers about interruptions, so ask your building manager for the exact day. Rīgas siltums advises running rarely used taps and showers for a few minutes at least once a week; this is worth doing after a holiday too.

In a renovated building, shared pipes are the building’s decision

The building at Elvīras iela 5 is on the Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks list of renovated buildings. In such a building the risers, heating pipes and towel rail supplies are usually common property, and the owners are jointly responsible for managing the building (Residential Property Management Law, Section 9).

If the riser section in your flat leaks or is rusted, replacing the section needs the building manager’s permission and the water shut off for the whole riser. The cost of replacing shared risers is usually covered by the flat owner according to their share and the owners' association’s decision (Apartment Ownership Law, Section 13).

For a radiator the procedure is longer. Rīgas siltums states that before replacement a heating designer prepares documentation with a heat loss calculation and agrees it with the building manager; as a general working window it gives roughly 1 May to 1 October. Your building manager sets the dates and the permission, because they shut off and drain the riser. In winter the work is usually postponed.

Dune sand between Gregora iela and Kalnciema iela

The south-western part of Zasulauks between Gregora iela, Dreiliņu iela and Kalnciema iela stands on the Zasulauks dunes, which were partly dug away for construction; originally they were 4–8 m high. For a flat on an upper floor this does not matter. It matters for a house with its own incoming supply pipe or a garden tap pipe across the yard.

Sandy ground freezes deeper than clay. Latvian building code LBN 003-19 allows the clay value to be used for sandy ground with a coefficient of 1.2: in Riga that is about 144 cm once in 100 years. If a pipe in the yard is laid shallower, in winter it is protected with thermal insulation or a heating cable, and the garden tap line is drained in autumn.

Questions residents ask

Does a heat network test shut off hot water across the whole of Zasulauks?

In its announcement, Rīgas siltums named the Zasulauks residential area, supplied by the Zasulauks and Imanta heat substations. Which buildings and on which day, Rīgas siltums tells the building managers, so ask your building manager.

What water heater should go in a Zasulauks flat that usually has hot water?

A backup water heater needs only a small volume if you use it only on interruption days. What matters more is where it will hang: on a solid wall, close to the water supplies, and with room for the safety valve drain, which points down and is not plugged.

Why can a radiator not be replaced in January?

In an apartment block a radiator is on a shared riser. For replacement the riser must be shut off and drained, and the building manager does this: they set the dates and the permission. As a general window, Rīgas siltums gives roughly 1 May to 1 October, and before that documentation agreed with the building manager is needed.
