Privacy policy - what happens to your request data

This page describes how the website santehnikisilguciems.lv uses the data you enter in the request form. The site has no user accounts and no payments, and the only way your data reaches it is the form.

Who is responsible for the data

The service provider that runs this website and passes the request to a plumbing specialist is responsible for processing the data. You can ask questions about your data through the form on the contact form page, stating in the message that the question is about data.

The form fields that are sent

  • name;
  • phone number;
  • district;
  • address;
  • type of problem;
  • urgency;
  • the message you write yourself.

In the message, please write only what the job needs. Do not give health data, a personal identity number or other information that a plumbing job does not need.

Why the data is needed

The data is needed so that the specialist can contact you, talk through the job and the price and, if you agree, come to the address given. The legal basis is steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation).

The route from the form to the specialist

A submitted form goes into a request-handling system, which passes it to the plumbing specialist who takes the job. Technically the request goes through the website’s server and a request automation service; the server may store the request briefly so that it can be sent again if the first attempt fails. We do not sell the data and do not use it for advertising.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical data for security, such as the IP address, the browser type and the time of the request.

Cookies

We do not set cookies on the website and do not use visit tracking for advertising.

How long the data stays

Request data is kept as long as needed to get in touch, do the job and answer questions about it, and as long as legislation requires, for example accounting requirements, if the job has taken place.

What you can ask for

  • access to your data and a copy of it;
  • correction of incorrect data;
  • deletion of data that is no longer needed;
  • restriction of processing, or an objection to it;
  • a complaint to the Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).

You can send a request through the form on the contact form page. The policy was updated on 16.11.2026.

Sources
