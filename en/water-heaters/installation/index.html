The price includes:

Charged separately:

  • a new water pipe to the water heater’s position (from €10/m);

  • a new electrical supply and a switch for the water heater;

Six measurements and connections at the wall

  1. Clearance to the ceiling The manufacturer (Gorenje) recommends leaving enough space above the water heater to replace the magnesium anode without taking the heater down: for TGR models 130 mm for 50 litres, 180 mm for 80 litres and 260 mm for 100 litres.
  2. Space for the service cover The manufacturer (Thermex) requires at least 30 cm from the protective cover to the nearest surface along the axis of the removable flange. If the cover butts against a toilet or a washing machine, the water heater will have to be taken down later for descaling.
  3. Wall and screws Gorenje TGR water heaters may only be mounted vertically, with screws of at least 8 mm, and a weak wall must be reinforced. Electrolux requires a mounting place that bears at least twice the weight of a full water heater.
  4. Water pressure Thermex water heaters may be connected at 0.05 to 0.7 MPa. If the pressure is higher, a reducer is fitted before the safety valve. Thermex also requires a pre-filter on the cold water inlet no coarser than 200 µm.
  5. Where the valve water goes If there is nowhere to route the drips from the safety valve into the drains, the manufacturer (Gorenje) recommends a 3-litre expansion vessel on the cold water inlet.
  6. Cable and switch Gorenje requires a supply cable of at least 1.5 mm² cross-section and a switch that disconnects all poles. The UK HSE states that a residual current device of no more than 30 mA can save a life, especially in damp rooms.

The numbers to enter in the form

  • The water heater volume in litres and whether it is already bought (the model from the box).
  • The distance from the planned top of the water heater to the ceiling, in centimetres.
  • The wall material: brick, concrete panel or plasterboard.
  • The room: the toilet (above the toilet bowl), bathroom, kitchen or the boiler room of a private house.
  • Whether there is a socket nearby or a separate supply.
  • The floor and whether the old water heater is still on the wall.
  • A photo of the wall, the water supply pipes and the valves.

Horizontal water heater under the ceiling or vertical on the wall

A vertical water heater takes less wall width but needs height: the clearance to the ceiling for the anode replacement is added to the casing itself. A horizontal one goes where there is no height, for example in a narrow toilet above the door or under the ceiling in a hallway.

Not every water heater can be rotated. The Gorenje TGR manual allows them to be mounted only vertically, so for a horizontal position you buy a model designed for it and check this in the manual before buying.

Replacing a water heater: pipes at the outlet and the old connection

When a water heater is replaced, the old pipes connected to it often stay in place. The manufacturer (Gorenje) states that if the working thermostat fails, the water in the tank can reach as much as 130 °C, and the pipework must withstand such an overload. Plastic systems have their own limit: TECEflex works at 95 °C for short periods but must never exceed 100 °C. So the material right at the water heater outlet is chosen from the data of both manufacturers.

A small water heater under the kitchen sink

Under a sink, a pressureless, or open-outlet, water heater is often fitted. The manufacturer (Gorenje) states that it serves only one tap, and a non-return valve and a special mixer tap are needed. When the water warms up and expands, the tap drips. This cannot be stopped by tightening the tap harder: tightening only damages it.

Questions residents ask

How much space to leave above the water heater in a toilet?

It depends on the model. Gorenje TGR gives 130–260 mm depending on volume, so that the anode can be replaced without taking the heater down. Check the manual for your model and enter the distance to the ceiling in the form.

How do I know whether our building needs a pressure reducer?

A pressure gauge on the cold water inlet shows the pressure. If it exceeds the limit in the water heater’s manual (Thermex: 0.7 MPa), a reducer is fitted before the safety valve.

Can the water heater be connected to the existing socket?

It depends on the socket and the cable. Gorenje requires a cable of at least 1.5 mm² and a switch that disconnects all poles. If there is no such supply, the electrical part is separate work.
