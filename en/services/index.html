When to report first to someone other than a plumber

  • No hot water in the whole building in summer: Iļģuciems and the Grīva area are connected to the Imanta and Zasulauks heating station networks, and during the hydraulic test of these networks there is no hot water across the district. Ask your building manager; more in the advice article no hot water in the flat.
  • Drains blocked in several flats, or water rising on the lower floor: the fault is usually in the riser or the horizontal drain, and the building manager needs to know.
  • Water coming out in the street or on the lawn before the meter chamber: under the Law on Water Management Services, the water utility maintains the network up to the ownership boundary, and in Riga that is Rīgas ūdens.

In a private house: the yard line, the incoming pipe and your own water heater

In a private house, for example in the “Spilves pļavas” estate in Kleisti, the yard is added to the systems. The house owner is usually responsible for the incoming supply pipe from the meter chamber to the house, for the yard sewer line and for the water heater, which is often the only source of hot water. Cleaning the yard line costs from €50, laying water pipe from €10/m, and hanging and connecting a water heater from €70.
