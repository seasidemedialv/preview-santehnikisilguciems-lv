Flushing the heating system in the detached-house streets of Kleisti, Voleri, Spilve and Iļģuciems is the homeowner’s decision, because the circuit from the boiler to the last radiator belongs to them.

In a block of flats, the radiators of one flat are not flushed separately: they are part of the building’s shared system, and the building manager decides. The price depends on the number of radiators, the chosen method and whether the boiler’s heat exchanger also needs cleaning.

The flushing job: what is and is not included

  • Included: inspection of the circuit and an estimate of its volume from the number of radiators and pipes.
  • Included: pouring in the cleaning solution or flushing the circuit with a flow of water, radiator by radiator.
  • Included: draining the dirty water, refilling with clean water, bleeding the air.
  • Not included: the cleaning solution and the protective fluid (inhibitor), which are materials.
  • Not included: cleaning the boiler’s internal heat exchanger; if it is needed, it is assessed separately.
  • Not included: replacing rusted valves or radiators found during flushing.

Flushing with a solution, step by step

  1. Volume and dose The circuit volume in litres is estimated from the number of radiators and the length of pipe. The amount of solution follows from that.
  2. Radiator valves open For Danfoss RA-N valves, the manufacturer gives the factory setting N (fully open) as the flushing position. Before flushing, the previous setting number of each valve is written down.
  3. Circulation The solution circulates together with the heating water, and the boiler runs as usual. The time depends on the contamination: from a few hours to several weeks.
  4. Rinsing out The dirty solution is drained and the circuit is flushed with clean water, radiator by radiator, until the water runs clear.
  5. Inhibitor and settings The circuit is refilled with clean water and protective fluid, the air is bled, and each valve gets its written-down setting back, from 1 to 7.

Sentinel X400 (Watts): circulate for at least 2 hours, in a heavily contaminated system up to 4 weeks. About 1 l is enough for a system up to about 95 l, for a larger one dose 1% of the volume; flush the system before adding the inhibitor. Danfoss RA-N: settings 1–7, N is fully open and suits flushing. Checked 16.11.2026.

Number of radiators, the boiler and the colour of the water

  • How many radiators the house has and on how many floors, and whether there are underfloor heating loops.
  • The make and model of the boiler or heat pump.
  • Which radiators are warm only at the top and which are completely cold.
  • What water comes out of the bleed valve: clear, brown or black.
  • When the circuit was last flushed or refilled from scratch, if you know.
  • Photos: the boiler with its pressure gauge and one radiator with its valve.

Balancing the heating system after flushing

If all valves are opened to N for flushing, the radiators farthest away afterwards run weaker than those closest to the boiler. That is why each valve gets its previous setting back. If nobody wrote the settings down, the circuit is balanced again from the radiator output and the distance to the boiler.

In a block of flats with a one-pipe riser, the Danfoss RA-N valves should already be on N anyway, and flushing the building’s system is a job at building level. If a radiator in a series-block flat is warm only at the top, start with radiator repair or report it to your building manager.

Flushing price and one example

There is no fixed figure for flushing a private house’s heating system. The price depends on how many radiators and loops the system has, whether it is flushed with a flow of water or with a cleaning solution, whether the boiler’s heat exchanger also needs cleaning, and what the protective fluid and the refilling cost.

Example: during flushing it turns out that one radiator is rusted inside. Replacing it is added to the flushing, from €75 per unit, labour only; materials are extra. Whether the trip is charged separately, the plumber tells you on the call before the work.

Questions residents ask

How long does the cleaning solution stay in the heating water?

Sentinel gives at least 2 hours of circulation for its X400; in a heavily contaminated system it can be left in normal operation for up to 4 weeks. After that the circuit is flushed with clean water.

Do the radiator valves need readjusting after flushing?

Yes, if they were opened to N for flushing. Each valve gets its previous setting back, otherwise the radiators farther from the boiler run weaker.

How much cleaning solution does our house’s circuit need?

For a system up to about 95 l, about 1 l of Sentinel X400 is enough. For a larger system the dose is 1% of the volume: for a circuit of about 150 l that is about 1.5 l. The volume is estimated from the number of radiators and the length of pipe.
