Washing machine connection - tap, drain and a stable place

If the tap and the drain are already in place, the work is from €35. If either is missing, a new connection point is made first.

Work from €35 and what comes on top

  • Included: connecting the outlet hose to a ready drain.

From the tap to the first programme

  1. A tap with a 3/4 inch thread AEG (Electrolux group) connects the inlet hose to a cold water tap with a 3/4 inch thread, and the incoming water must not be warmer than 25 °C.
  2. A straight run below the tap Below the tap at least 200 mm of straight run is left for the hose.
  3. A vented outlet end The inner diameter of the standpipe must be at least 38 mm and larger than the outer diameter of the hose, so that the end is vented. The outlet hose can be extended up to 400 cm.
  4. Level on a solid floor The machine is placed on a level, solid floor and levelled with the adjustable feet.
  5. Water back slowly WaterSafe advises restoring the water slowly, inspecting the joints and inspecting them again after 24 hours.

The pipes knock when the machine takes water

A washing machine opens and shuts the water with a solenoid valve. Caleffi explains that when the flow stops suddenly, water hammer occurs, which can burst pipes and hoses, wear seals and cause noise.

In the manufacturer’s example, in a 10 m long 1/2 inch pipe at a flow of 2 m/⁠s, a sudden closing gives a pressure surge of about 28.5 bar in a copper pipe and 18 bar in a PE-X pipe. In plastic pipes the shock is smaller, yet Caleffi recommends an absorber there even more, and fits it as close as possible to the device that shuts the flow.

Machine, room and building: facts for the request

  • The machine’s maker and model, freestanding or built-in.
  • The room, and whether the tap and the drain are already there; if not, how many metres to the nearest pipe.
  • The building type and floor: Iļģuciems has both five-storey series 464 and nine-storey series 602 panel blocks.
  • Whether the pipes already knock when a tap is closed.
  • Photographs: the machine’s place, the tap and the drain inlet.

Dishwasher connection under the kitchen trap

Beko requires a water pressure from the tap of 0.3 to 10 bar for its dishwasher; above 10 bar a pressure-reducing valve is fitted in between. The outlet hose connection to the drain or the sink trap must be 50–100 cm above the floor.

After connecting, the tap is opened fully and you check that nothing leaks. The manufacturer advises closing the tap after each programme. This is the same row of the price list as for a washing machine.

A new place for the machine: an example

Connecting a machine to a ready tap and drain is from €35 per unit, one new connection point from €35. Example: in a bathroom both a new water supply and a drain outlet are needed, then the machine itself: 2 × from €35 + from €35 = at least €105 labour only; materials are extra.

Questions residents ask

The inlet hose does not reach the tap. Can I add an extension?

AEG states that a hose that is too short is not extended but replaced with a longer one. The outlet hose is a different case: it can be extended up to 400 cm.

The floor is sloping. Can I put cardboard or a small board under a foot?

AEG does not recommend it: the machine is levelled with the adjustable feet, not with cardboard, wood or similar materials.

The dishwasher started washing worse. Is the connection to blame?

Beko states that the inlet hose filter traps sand, dirt and rust, so it is checked and cleaned regularly. Worse washing is also caused by an outlet hose longer than 4 m.
