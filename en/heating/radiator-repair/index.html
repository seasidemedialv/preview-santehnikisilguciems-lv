Dripping under the thermostatic head: the repair step by step

  1. The head comes off Without the head, the valve stem and its seal are visible. If the stem is wet, the fault is the stem seal; if the nut at the pipe is wet, the fault is the joint.
  2. The stem seal is replaced without draining the water Danfoss states for its RA-N valves that the whole stem seal unit can be replaced without draining the system. For other models, check the marking on the body.
  3. The nut is resealed If tightening the nut is not enough, the radiator is shut off with both valves. If there are no valves or they do not hold, the riser has to be shut off, and that is the building manager’s decision.
  4. Checked in the hot system After the work the head is put back and the joint is inspected once the radiator has warmed up again.

Danfoss RA-N data sheet: the stem seal unit can be replaced without draining the system; the red protective cap must not be used as a shut-off device. Checked 16.11.2026.

The thermostatic head shows 3 and the room is cool

For its K head, the manufacturer IMI Heimeier states that number 3 corresponds to about 20 °C, with about 4 °C between adjacent numbers and about 1 °C between the marks. The manufacturer does not recommend a setting above 4, because each extra degree in a room uses about 6% more energy.

If the head on 3 does not warm the room, first look at what covers it. A head with a built-in sensor must not be covered with a curtain or a radiator cover, fitted vertically or put in a narrow niche: then it measures its own heat, not the room’s. Such a place needs a head with a remote sensor.

Fitting a thermostatic head on an existing valve body does not require draining the water. Installing thermostatic valves does change the valve body, so in a block of flats it is done on a date set by the building manager, when the riser is empty.

The valve whistles or hisses

For quiet operation of Danfoss RA-N valves, the pressure drop across the valve is recommended not to exceed 30–35 kPa; the maximum pressure difference is 0.6 bar. Whistling when the head is almost closed can mean a large pressure difference in the building’s system. It is regulated at the heat substation, so it should be reported to the building manager, not fixed by replacing the valve.

Details of the faulty radiator

  • Where it drips: under the head, at the nut, from the body or from a seam between sections.
  • The marking on the valve or head (make, model).
  • Whether the radiator has valves on both sides.
  • The floor, and whether it is a block of flats or a private house.
  • A close-up photo and a photo of the whole radiator.

Bleeding radiators before you call

If a radiator is warm only at the bottom and cool at the top, it is not a repair but air. You can usually bleed it yourself; see the advice on bleeding radiators. If in a private house the bottom part stays cold, heating system flushing helps.

Questions residents ask

Can a radiator be shut off with the red plastic cap?

No. Danfoss states that the red protective cap of the RA-N valve can be used for regulating during construction work, but not as a shut-off device. A radiator closed with the cap is not cut off from the riser, and it must not be removed.

Can a thermostatic head in a cellar be set to the frost-protection symbol?

Yes. For the IMI Heimeier K head the setting range is 6–28 °C, and the manufacturer recommends the frost-protection setting specifically for cellar rooms. For heads with a zero position, the valve opens at about 0 °C.

A seam between the sections of a cast-iron radiator drips. Is it repaired in winter?

A seam between sections is repaired with the radiator taken apart, so with an empty riser. In winter, collect the drops in a container and tell the building manager; the work itself or a radiator replacement is done when the building manager allows the riser to be drained.
