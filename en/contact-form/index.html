The form fields and what to write in them

  • Name and phone: the specialist calls this number.
  • District: Iļģuciems, one of the 12 nearby neighbourhoods, or “Other”.
  • Address: street, building and flat; for a private house, also how to get into the yard.
  • Type of problem: choose the closest from the list (emergency, pipe leak, drains, tap, water heater, bathroom) or “Other” and describe it yourself.
  • Urgency: whether it is needed right away, within this week or later on a day agreed.
  • Message: the floor, the type of building (five-storey panel, brick, nine-storey, private house), what exactly is happening and since when.

A few words that save questions on the call

If something leaks, write where from: a tap, a joint in the toilet duct, the ceiling. If a fixture is to be replaced, write whether the new one is already bought, and its size. If the job involves a riser or a radiator in a multi-flat building, note whether the building manager already knows. If water is leaking right now, first close the valve in the flat and only then fill in the form: the emergency plumber page describes the first steps.

Two more facts help for a private house: whether the water comes from the city network through a meter pit, and whether the drains go to a city inspection chamber or a holding tank. This shows at once which pipe is yours.

Questions residents ask

Why can I not simply phone here?

Requests are accepted only through the form. With it the request already has the address, the district and the problem, and the specialist calls you already knowing what it is about. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

In which language can I fill in the form?

In Latvian, English or Russian. On the call you can continue in the same language.

Does a request commit me to anything?

No. On the call you talk through the job and the price, and only then decide whether the plumber comes. Prices are public on the plumbing prices page.
