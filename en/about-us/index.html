Three types of building on one bank

On this bank, one route has very different buildings. Iļģuciems has series 303 brick blocks from the 1950s, five-storey large-panel estates and nine-storey blocks, Imanta and Zolitūde have even taller panel blocks, and in Kleisti, Spilve and Voleri people live in private houses. Each has its own plumbing: in one case a boxed-in riser duct in the toilet with the risers, in the other an incoming pipe from a meter pit and its own boiler.

What to write about your building in the message

  • Series block: the floor, and whether the job involves the riser in the toilet duct. The riser is common property of the building and needs the building manager’s permission and the water shut off for the whole riser, so you sort this out on the call before the visit.
  • Newer multi-flat building: whether the pipes are in the wall or in a box, and where the flat’s valves are.
  • Private house: where the meter pit is. Rīgas ūdens states that the owner’s responsibility usually starts at the outer edge of the pit wall, so the incoming pipe and the yard line are on the house’s side.

The form has a field for the district and for the message: they show before the call whether the job is a toilet in a five-storey block or the yard line of a private house.

Jobs by system

  • Water pipes: leaks, pipes in a flat and on the incoming supply pipe of a private house, riser sections with the building manager’s permission.
  • Drains: blockages in a flat, yard lines, high-pressure jetting.
  • Water heaters: installation, repair and descaling.
  • Heating: radiators, heated towel rails, flushing a private house’s system.
  • Fixtures: toilets, sinks, mixer taps, shower cabins, baths, washing machines and dishwashers.

Prices and requests

All jobs with starting prices are on the plumbing prices page, for example a call-out from €40 and an hourly rate from €25/h. They cover labour only, and materials are extra. On the call after the form you talk through the job and the price for your case, and only then does the plumber come.

There is no phone number on the page: everything starts with the form on the contact form page. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. You can write in Latvian, English or Russian. If you do not yet know which job is needed, start with the plumbing services page or with the advice section.
