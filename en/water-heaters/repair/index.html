Water heater repair - when it switches off, does not heat or runs weakly

Water heater repair in west Riga starts with a question: has the overheating protection tripped (manufacturers call it a thermal cut-out or safety thermostat), or is the fault in the safety valve on the water side?

The protection switches off the heating, but the valve does not stop the water heater: it only releases excess pressure, and a blocked valve reduces the hot water flow. A repair is done when the tank is intact and the part is accessible; if the tank leaks, a new water heater is the better choice.

What a repair includes and does not include

Included:

  • finding the faulty part;
  • replacing one part: heating element, thermostat, anode or safety valve;
  • draining and refilling the water heater, if the part cannot be reached otherwise.

Not included:

  • the spare parts themselves;
  • removing limescale from the tank, which is water heater descaling;
  • repairing the electrical supply up to the water heater;
  • a new water heater, if the tank leaks.

Finding the fault from the outside in

  1. Power Check that power reaches the water heater and that the residual current device has not tripped. If the device switches off just when the water heater starts heating, the fault may be in the heating element or the wiring.
  2. Overheating protection Electrolux calls it a safety thermostat. In the manufacturer’s description it is reset only with the water heater disconnected from the power, the service panel opened with a tool and then closed.
  3. Working thermostat If the protection keeps switching off again and again, the working thermostat may be faulty. The manufacturer (Gorenje) states that in that case the water in the tank can reach even 130 °C, so such a water heater is not kept in use.
  4. Water side Compare the hot and cold water flow and inspect the safety valve on the inlet.
  5. Tank If water seeps from the body itself rather than from a joint, the tank cannot be repaired.

What to state in the form about the faulty water heater

  • The make, model and capacity from the label.
  • How many years the water heater has been running.
  • What happens: lukewarm water, no heating at all, switching off, tripping the device, leaking.
  • Whether the safety thermostat has already been reset and how many times.
  • Whether the water heater is new and whether it was carried in a car in winter.
  • A photo of the water heater, the valve and the place under it.

Water heater not heating: repair or a new one

If the tank is intact, replacing the heating element, thermostat or valve is usually less work than a new water heater. A repair does not pay off if the tank leaks through the body, if the model no longer has spare parts, or if the part and the labour together approach the price of a new water heater.

In the centre and south of Zolitūde, detached houses dominate. In such a house the water heater is the only source of hot water, and the decision on a repair or a new water heater has to be made within a day. A flat with the building’s hot water has time to compare both options.

Questions residents ask

The new water heater does not heat after installation. Is it faulty?

Not always. The manufacturer (Stiebel Eltron) states that the high-temperature fuse can trip if the water heater has been below -15 °C in transport or storage. It is reset with a button. If it still does not heat after that, another cause has to be found.

Can I use a water heater that switches itself off now and then?

It is not recommended. Repeated tripping means the water reaches the protection limit. Thermex names a temperature set too close to the limit or limescale on the thermostat tube as causes, and Gorenje states that with a faulty working thermostat the water can reach 130 °C. Disconnect the water heater from the power and request an inspection.

Why does hot water from the water heater run weakly while cold runs normally?

In the Thermex manual, this sign points to a blocked safety valve on the cold water inlet. The valve is cleaned or replaced.
