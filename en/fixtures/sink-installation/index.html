Under the sink: connections checked one by one

  1. Fixing to the wall or worktop The Iļģuciems residential district was planned in 1968 from five-storey large-panel sections, but the neighbourhood also has brick blocks of series 303 and 316/318. The plug and screw differ for concrete, brick and a thin partition, so the wall is identified before drilling.
  2. Threaded joints The manufacturer TECE recommends flax fibre together with an approved paste for sealing threads. Too much flax can damage the thread, and after tightening the thread ends must stay visible.
  3. Compression joints If a compression nut leaks, WaterSafe, the register of British plumbers, advises tightening it carefully by a quarter turn while holding the tap body still.
  4. Trap and drain The trap is connected to the drain, the sink is filled and emptied. Then each joint is checked from below with dry paper.
  5. Second check after 24 hours After a repair WaterSafe advises restoring the water slowly, checking the joints at once and again after 24 hours. You can do this yourself with the same paper.

Measurements to know before you buy a sink

  • the width and depth of the sink, and whether it has an overflow;
  • in a bathroom: the wall material and the height from the floor to the existing drain;
  • in a kitchen: the worktop thickness and whether the cut-out already exists;
  • whether a dishwasher or washing machine will connect at the sink;
  • the building type and floor;
  • a photo under the old sink showing the valves and the drain pipe.

Kitchen sink installation with a dishwasher next to it

A kitchen sink trap often takes the dishwasher water as well. The manufacturer Beko states that the drain hose connection to the drain or to the sink trap must be 50–100 cm above the floor, and a hose longer than 4 m makes washing worse. So a trap with a branch is chosen already when the sink is bought.

A single-lever mixer tap shuts the water quickly. The manufacturer Caleffi explains that a sudden stop of flow causes water hammer, which you hear as a knock in the pipes. In plastic pipes it advises closing taps more slowly and fitting the shock absorber as close as possible to the device that shuts the flow.

Jaunpiebalga municipality reminds residents that fat, oil, vegetable and fruit peelings and paper kitchen towels must not get into the drains.

Trap replacement when the sink stays

A cracked or leaking trap can be replaced without touching the sink. If the trap is blocked, the Swedish Chemicals Agency advises trying mechanical methods first, by hand, with a rubber plunger or a coil, rather than a caustic cleaner.

If a cleaner has already been poured in and the blockage remained, tell the plumber, because such products contain sodium or potassium hydroxide. For replacing a trap the hourly rate from €25/h applies, and clearing a blockage in a flat is from €50.

Questions residents ask

Does the dishwasher valve under the sink have to stay open all the time?

For safety Beko advises closing the tap fully after each wash programme. When connecting the machine, use the new inlet hose from the set, not the old one, and then open the tap fully to check for leaks.

After the new tap, the pipes knock when I shut the water. What is it?

It can be water hammer from a quickly closed flow. Caleffi states that it can wear seals and valves and damage hoses. Close the lever more slowly; if the knock stays, a shock absorber can be fitted at the tap.

Drain cleaner has already been poured into the trap. What should I do before the plumber comes?

Pour nothing more in and tell the plumber which product was used. If it gets into the eyes, the US National Poison Center advises rinsing them at once with running water for at least 20 minutes.
