Which is better depends on your list. Replacing one part is clearer by its own row, but several short maintenance jobs are cheaper combined into one hour, because the minimum paid time is usually 1 hour.

What the hour covers and what is charged separately

Included in the hourly rate:

  • work on your list that can be done with the existing supply pipes;
  • removing a part, cleaning it and refitting it, or replacing it with a new one;
  • turning the water back on and checking the joints after the work.

Not included:

  • spare parts: aerators, fill valves, membranes, gaskets;
  • jobs that need a wall or tiles opened;
  • work on the shared riser: it is the building’s common property, and the building manager organises it.

Plumber for small jobs by the hour: a list from the manufacturers' manuals

Many small jobs are maintenance that the manufacturers themselves describe in their manuals. They are the same in an old brick house and in a five-storey large-panel block, the kind Iļģuciems’s residential areas were planned with from 1968: taps, toilet, filters.

  • Toilet leaks after flushing: the manufacturer (Geberit) names dirt or limescale lodged where the discharge valve gasket meets the outlet opening as the usual cause. The valve is taken out, cleaned with water or a dry cloth and put back.
  • Cistern fills slowly: the manufacturer (Ideal Standard) recommends cleaning the fill valve filter regularly, and Wirquin recommends taking out and washing the membrane and the filter during maintenance.
  • The tap’s stream has become weak or splashes sideways: the manufacturer (Neoperl) states that limescale and deposits collect in the aerator. It is cleaned or replaced when the limescale is visible or the stream is uneven.
  • A leaking joint under the sink: the UK plumbers' register WaterSafe recommends tightening the compression nut by a quarter turn while holding the tap body still.

How one hour’s work is put together, step by step

  1. A check before the request The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA WaterSense) test: drop food colouring into the cistern. If after 10 minutes the colour is in the toilet bowl, the cistern leaks. Write the result in the list.
  2. The call about the pricing system On the call you talk through the job and the price: which job goes by its own row, which by the hour and which parts must be bought beforehand.
  3. Water shut off once The jobs that need the water shut off are done one after another while the flat’s valves are closed, and only then is the water turned back on.
  4. Your second check after 24 hours WaterSafe recommends turning the water back on slowly and inspecting the joints again after 24 hours. You do this second check yourself: a dry paper towel under a joint shows even a small drip.

What to note against each line of the list

  • The room and the fixture: kitchen, bathroom or separate toilet.
  • Whether the toilet cistern sits on the toilet or is built into the wall, and the manufacturer, if visible on the cover or the button.
  • The food colouring test result and the minutes after which the colour appeared.
  • A photo of the tap from below and the end of the aerator.
  • The floor and whether the parts are already bought.

Two bills for one list

Example: the toilet leaks, and the kitchen tap has a weak stream. If the toilet’s mechanism is replaced, that is a toilet flush mechanism repair from €35, and cleaning the aerator goes at the hourly rate from €25/h: at least €60 together. If cleaning the flush valve is enough, both jobs are maintenance and are then paid by the hour only, at least €25 for the first hour.

If you only need an inspection and an answer to what is faulty, the plumber’s call-out and inspection costs from €40. Whether the trip is charged separately, the plumber tells you on the call before the work. All amounts are labour only; materials are extra.

Questions residents ask

The job took 20 minutes. How much time do I pay for?

The minimum paid time is usually 1 hour. That is why it is cheaper to collect small jobs in one list than to request each separately.

Do I need a written contract for small jobs?

The Consumer Rights Protection Centre (PTAC) explains that a written contract is not mandatory, but it makes proof easier in a dispute. An oral agreement is also a contract, but in a dispute you must prove both it and the payment, so correspondence and a signed confirmation of payment are useful.

The colour did not appear in the toilet, but it is damp under the cistern. What is leaking?

The food colouring test only shows whether the cistern leaks into the toilet bowl. Dampness on the floor comes from elsewhere: the supply connection or the gasket between the cistern and the toilet. In the form, write exactly where it is damp and attach a photo.
