Riser replacement in a flat - the building’s decision, work in your toilet duct

Riser replacement in a flat in Riga’s west-side series blocks is common-property work that happens inside your boxed-in toilet duct.

When the building manager shuts off the riser, every flat on it is left without water: usually five in a five-storey series 464 block, nine in a nine-storey series 602 block. A water riser section on one floor costs from €110, and a drain section from €100.

What the section price covers and what comes on top

  • Covered: a length of water riser within the bounds of one flat.
  • Covered: a length of cast-iron or old plastic drain riser on one floor, with transitions to the neighbouring sections.
  • Covered: inspecting the joints once the riser has water again.
  • Extra: opening and closing the duct, and tiling (separate work).
  • Extra: the flat’s drain pipe up to the riser, from €30 per connection point.
  • Extra: new water pipes beyond the flat’s valves, from €10/m.
  • Extra: pipes, fittings and fixings.
  • Not our work: shutting off and draining the riser, which the building manager does.

The work in three steps

  1. Decision and day The flat owner notifies the building manager of the work. The building manager sets the day when the riser will be shut off and warns the neighbours.
  2. Work on an empty riser The building manager closes and drains the riser, then the old length is cut out and a new one fitted with transitions above and below.
  3. Water back on The riser is refilled, the joints are inspected, and only then is the duct closed, as separate work.

Details about the building to enter in the form

  • The building’s series or number of floors: series 464 (5), series 602 (9), series 104 (12–16), Czech design (12).
  • Your floor and which pipes: cold water, hot water, drains.
  • The material: steel, cast iron, plastic.
  • Whether the duct has an access hatch and how high the ceiling in the toilet is.
  • Whether the building manager has agreed and whether the shut-off day is known.
  • Whether the building plans to replace all the risers.
  • Photos: the duct from the front, a leak or rust on the riser.

A cast-iron soil stack

Inside an old cast-iron riser, grease and limescale settle and the pipe gets narrower. The manufacturer REMS calls the chain cleaner the key tool for removing grease deposits and limescale, and states that for cast-iron pipes chains with pointed links are used, for plastic ones with smooth rings. If the pipe is sound, it can be cleaned; if the cast iron has a crack or a socket leaks, the section is replaced.

A blocked riser affects the flats at the bottom first. An LSM article states that wipes and other waste in the drains of blocks of flats cause blocked pipes, smells and waste water backing up into the rooms.

When the building replaces the whole riser

Article 13 of the Apartment Ownership Law provides that a flat owner pays for replacing common-property utilities in proportion to their share, following a decision of the flat owners' association. For example, Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga’s municipal building manager) replaced the water and drain risers in 2019 in the building at Keramikas iela 2 in Bolderāja, and the basement horizontal pipes at Slokas iela 169 in Imanta.

Article 10 of the same law obliges a flat owner to let in specialists who do the work that keeps the building’s utilities functioning normally. If the duct is tiled over without a hatch, that is exactly what will have to be opened.

Drinking water on the day the riser is empty

A Rīgas ūdens free tap is in Nordeķu Park by Dzirciema iela 60. The Imanta free tap at Jūrmalas gatve 133 also works in winter, and another is by Anniņmuiža Park, at Anniņmuižas bulvāris 77.

Section prices and one calculation

Replacing a water riser section: from €110 per section on one floor. Replacing a drain riser section: from €100 per section on one floor. Replacing a flat’s drain pipe: from €30 per connection point.

Example: a hot water riser and a cast-iron drain on one floor, plus new drain connections for the toilet and the bath. That is from €110 and from €100 plus 2 × €30, at least €270 in total for labour, with materials extra. Whether the trip is charged separately, the plumber tells you on the call before the work.

Questions residents ask

What should I check during the first day after the new section is fitted?

After the work the water is returned to the riser gradually. WaterSafe recommends inspecting the joints at once and again after 24 hours, because a small leak often shows only after some time. In the toilet duct, look at the ceiling and floor transitions where the new section meets the old one.

Who checks the condition of the building’s risers?

The building manager, by surveying the building. LV portāls (Latvia’s official legal information portal) states that from 1 January 2025 the standard LVS 1077:2023 must be applied in surveys of blocks of flats.

Can the pipes be hidden after the work with a boxed section under the ceiling?

In new building and reconstruction, LBN 200-21 sets the minimum height to the ceiling or to a utility cover box in sanitary rooms at 2.2 m. That applies to a horizontal box under the ceiling, not to the vertical riser duct in the corner of the toilet. If a horizontal box would be lower, it is built differently.
