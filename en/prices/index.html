What makes up the final total

The table gives the lowest price for a simple case. The total goes up with access (a pipe under tiles or in a toilet duct that must be opened), with parts that are not on site, with work on the building’s common property, which needs the building manager’s permission, and with connecting several points at once. Taking away an old bath is a separate job, and in a five-storey block without a lift the bath is carried down the stairs.

Materials, such as a new toilet, a mixer tap or a radiator, are bought separately. On the call before the work you talk through the job and the price.

The hourly rate: small jobs in one visit

The plumber’s hourly rate is from €25/h, and the minimum charged time is usually 1 hour. The hourly rate suits small, varied jobs: replacing a stiff shower hose, fixing a loose sink bracket, freeing a stiff valve. A visit for several jobs is described on the plumber for small jobs page.

Questions residents ask

Why is water heater repair not priced in the table?

Because the price depends on which part is faulty and whether spare parts exist for it. Sometimes a repair costs more than a new water heater, and that can only be seen on site.

Is the riser section price for the whole building?

No. Replacing a water riser section from €110 and a soil stack section from €100 is priced per section on one floor, within one flat.
