In its plan, Rīgas ūdens (Riga’s water utility) names Imanta first among the neighbourhoods with the most water main failures, but the horizontal pipes in the cellar and the risers are the building’s common property, decided together with the building manager. For a flat in a building of two to sixteen floors and for a detached house with its own supply pipe, this path differs.

Imanta borders Iļģuciems, and from its centre to Imanta it is about 3.7 km (our map reading). In a series flat the riser is shared by the whole vertical, while in a detached house the owner has everything between the ownership boundary and the tap.

Ground, the Lāčupīte collector and the building’s outlet

In Imanta building conditions are mostly favourable: only 3.5 ha around the Imanta heat plant and the Zasulauks depot need engineering preparation, while the weakest ground is in the south-east between Slokas iela, Kurzemes prospekts and Jūrmalas gatve. The middle reach of the Lāčupīte up to the northern part of Imanta is channelled into a reinforced concrete collector. If a yard pipe has to be relaid in weak ground or close to an underground structure, the plumber can judge the digging spot and the scope only on site.

Stage 5 of the Cohesion Fund project brought 10.3 km of water main, plus 10.8 km of sewer, to the streets of north-west Imanta, so 185 households could connect. A city main in the street does not yet mean the building is connected: the owner is responsible for the section from the ownership boundary to the building. For the building’s outlet from the cellar, LBN 221-15 requires a fall of no less than 20 ‰, that is 2 cm per metre, and the outlet cannot be narrower than the largest riser connected to it. A 100 mm outlet from the riser or cleanout to the axis of the inspection chamber may be no longer than 12 m, a 50 mm outlet no longer than 8 m.

The Rīgas ūdens boundary is usually set by the wall of the meter chamber (its outer side). On a plot without a chamber the boundary runs along the plot boundary or the street’s red line. Checked 16.11.2026.

High groundwater by Beberbeķu iela and Ķiburgas iela

Rīgas ūdens has pointed to a high groundwater level in the part of Imanta around Beberbeķu iela and Ķiburgas iela. Drainage ditches and culverts there must be maintained by the owner of the plot on whose land they lie.

A ditch and a culvert are not plumbing, and cleaning them is the owner’s job. The plumber’s part is the yard sewer line and its chambers. If after rain the drain in the house runs away more slowly, first look in the yard chamber: does the water stand higher than the pipe’s inlet. That shows whether the problem is in the line up to the chamber or further on.

A street water main failure is not a flat’s failure

The large number of street failures cited in the Rīgas ūdens long-term plan also means rebuilding work on streets. On Jūrmalas gatve, on the stretch from Zolitūdes iela to Imantas 7. līnija, Rīgas ūdens announced a 2.3 km water main reconstruction with a new low-pressure water main to supply Imanta and Beberbeķi.

If the water disappears in the whole building or street, a plumber in the flat will not restore it. Ask your building manager or watch the water supplier’s notices. When the water returns, the pipes may contain air and sediment: run the cold water until it becomes clear. If the tap then flows weakly, most likely the tap strainer is clogged; you can unscrew it and rinse it yourself.

A detached house’s line and a flat’s riser: where to start

For a detached house’s line there is drain cleaning and pipe repair. For a series flat, riser replacement and towel rail replacement fit. For a private house’s heating there is heating system flushing, and for a new washing machine connection washing machine connection.

Questions residents ask

The building manager replaced the horizontal pipes in the cellar. Do the drains in the flat have to be replaced now too?

No, the horizontal pipe and the flat’s drains are different parts. For example, in 2019 Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga’s municipal building manager) replaced the horizontal pipes in the cellar of the building at Slokas iela 169. The flat’s drains up to the riser are replaced if they leak or often block; that is from €30 per connection point.

The new outlet goes through the cellar wall. How is it sealed?

LBN 221-15 requires a 0.2 m gap to be left around the pipe in the wall or foundations and filled with a flexible material that is impermeable to water and gases. In wet ground a sealing sleeve is needed. This way the pipe can move slightly, and water does not enter the cellar through the gap.

Where can I get drinking water while the building’s riser is being replaced?

The Rīgas ūdens public drinking tap at Jūrmalas gatve 133, called “Imantas brīvkrāns”, works in winter too. Another tap is by Anniņmuižas parks, at Anniņmuižas bulvāris 77. Before the work you can also fill containers with water.
