When a house is lived in all year, the water pipe has to survive a winter that the summer system may not have been made for. From the centre of Iļģuciems to Kleisti it is about 4.7 km (our map reading).

The soil also differs between plots. Of the Kleisti manor’s 4,000 pūrvietas, 1,500 were forest and 500 were sandy dunes. So in two neighbouring yards the pipe depth and the position of the incoming supply pipe are assessed separately for each.

When a summer house starts to be lived in through winter

  1. Find the route of the outdoor pipe From the incoming supply pipe to the house. Building code LBN 222-15 requires the outdoor water pipe to be laid at least 0.5 m below the depth where the ground freezes; if the pipe lies shallower, it is moved deeper or protected with a heating cable.
  2. A heating cable with protection A self-regulating cable, for example the Danfoss DEVI model 10 V3, gives about 10 W per metre at 10 °C. The manufacturer requires a thermostat that protects against overheating and a residual-current switch that trips at up to 30 mA. The nVent Raychem FroStop cable also needs a 30 mA switch.
  3. Plastic pipes in the floor Uponor writes of its PEX pipes that one freeze usually does not burst them, but each further freeze weakens the material. If a PEX pipe is cast in concrete without a protective sleeve, ice can pierce it.
  4. The new section is tested before it is covered Once the pipes are in, they are pressure-tested and only then covered with a floor or wall (see the question below).

For taps and a shower that are rarely used in the house, AS “Rīgas siltums” advises running the water for a few minutes at least once a week, so that it does not stand in the pipes.

The way to the house and to the inspection chamber

The main streets in Kleisti are Kleistu iela and Rātsupītes iela, but most of the neighbourhood has no street network. A wide network of canals and ditches makes it hard to get around overgrown areas. In the form, write which street to drive in from, whether the van reaches the gate and how many metres it is from the gate to the house. For cleaning the yard sewer line, this decides whether the hose or cable will reach the place it needs to.

Under LBN 223-15, a yard sewer line needs an inspection chamber at every bend, junction and point where the slope or pipe size changes, and on a straight run with a pipe up to 200 mm every 35 m. In a green area the cover rises 20–70 mm above the ground. In an overgrown garden grass can grow over the cover, so find it and clear it before the visit.

Whether the yard sewer line ends in the city’s chamber or in your own tank has to be found out for each house separately. For your address, ask Rīgas ūdens, and in the form write what you know about it.

Questions residents ask

How do you check that the new water pipe does not leak before the floor is covered?

With water pressure. For its PE-Xa and multilayer pipe systems, Uponor specifies two stages. First 11 bar is held for 30 minutes and must not drop; then the test continues at 5.5 bar for 120 minutes. Expansion vessels and safety valves cannot take such pressure, so they are disconnected beforehand.

Can a heating cable be pulled inside the water pipe itself?

Some allow it. The manufacturer of the Danfoss DEVI cable DEVIpipeheat 10 V3 allows it to be laid inside the pipe too, including in Latvia, if the water is no warmer than 23 °C and the pressure does not exceed 10 bar. For other cables, see the manufacturer’s instructions.

What size of pit does a drainage pump with a float need in the cellar?

In its drawing for the Unilift KP pump, the manufacturer Grundfos gives minimum pit dimensions of about 356 mm and 407 mm, but the size is also chosen by how fast the water flows in. The float’s free cable should be 63.5–152 mm: a longer cable means fewer starts. A permanently installed pump must have a non-return valve on the outlet.
