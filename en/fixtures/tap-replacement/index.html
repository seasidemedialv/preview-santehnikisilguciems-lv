A dripping tap often needs only a new cartridge, but if you buy a new thermostatic tap, first check that it will work with your heater. Replacing a tap on the existing supply pipes is from €25.

What the tap replacement price covers

The price from €25 includes:

  • removing the old tap;
  • connecting the new tap to the existing supply pipes or wall outlets;
  • checking the temperature of a thermostatic tap and inspecting the joints with water running.

Charged separately:

  • the tap itself, a cartridge and hoses (materials);
  • a new supply or outlet if the tap has to move: from €35 per point;
  • a pressure reducer or a shock absorber, if they are needed: at the hourly rate from €25/h.

A thermostatic tap and your water heater

A thermostatic tap holds the set temperature by itself, but it needs suitable water. The manufacturer (Grohe) states these requirements for its thermostats:

  • the hot water at the inlet must be at least 2 °C warmer than the mixed temperature you want;
  • the minimum flow is 5 l/⁠min;
  • if the static pressure exceeds 5 bar, a pressure reducer must be fitted;
  • the thermostat cannot be used with unpressurised (open-vented) water heaters;
  • with an instantaneous heater it works only if the heater output is at least 18 kW.

Grohe thermostats are set at the factory at 3 bar flow pressure on both sides, and the safety stop is at 38 °C. If the pressure on site is different, the displayed temperature can differ, and the thermostat is readjusted. If the water is heated by the building’s heat substation or a pressurised water heater, the main questions are pressure and flow.

Replacing a tap step by step

  1. Close the flat’s valves Close the cold and hot water valves. A US water utility (Brunswick & Topsham Water District) states that older homes usually have gate valves with a metal plate, and newer ones have ball valves that close with a 90-degree turn. An old gate valve may not close fully.
  2. Remove the old tap On a wall tap the nuts are unscrewed from the eccentric fittings; on a sink or kitchen tap the flexible hoses and the fixing are unscrewed from below.
  3. Eccentrics or hoses On a wall tap you check that the eccentric fittings are intact and that the spacing of the new tap matches. On a kitchen tap the new hoses from the set are fitted.
  4. Water and temperature The water is turned on slowly and the joints are inspected. On a thermostatic tap the mixed temperature is checked and, if needed, adjusted.

Replacing a tap cartridge when the tap drips

In a lever tap the water is mixed and shut off by a ceramic cartridge. If the tap drips even when closed or the lever moves stiffly, replacing the cartridge is often enough, and the body stays in place. Replacing the whole tap makes sense if the body itself leaks, the tap moves at the sink, or a spare cartridge for that model can no longer be found. Grohe states that after each service of a thermostat cartridge the temperature must be set again.

In the oldest houses of Iļģuciems, for example the Grīvas iela housing estate built in 1958–1960, the tap may have been replaced several times, while the flat’s valves and wall outlets may still be the originals. Then it is worth inspecting them as well before replacing the tap.

A knock in the pipes after closing the lever

The manufacturer (Caleffi) explains that water hammer occurs when the water flow stops suddenly, for example when a single-lever mixer tap is closed. The shock can damage pipes and hoses, wear seals and valves and cause noise. In plastic pipes the pressure surge is smaller, but Caleffi advises closing taps more slowly there too, and fitting the shock absorber as close as possible to the device that shuts the flow quickly.

What to write about the tap and the water

  • Where the tap is: on the sink, on the edge of the bath or on the wall on eccentric fittings.
  • What heats the water: the building’s hot water, a pressurised water heater (litres) or an instantaneous heater (kW).
  • Which tap is to be fitted: lever or thermostatic, the manufacturer and the model.
  • Whether the flat’s valves close fully.
  • A photo from below or of the wall outlets.

Two taps at once: the calculation

A wall tap on eccentric fittings in the bathroom and a tap with hoses in the kitchen: 2 × from €25 = at least €50. If the kitchen tap has to move and needs a new supply, from €35 per point is added, at least €85 in total. Labour only; materials are extra. Questions residents ask

Can a thermostatic tap go with a small water heater under the kitchen sink?

It depends on the water heater. If it is an unpressurised (open-vented) heater, Grohe states that the thermostat cannot be used with it. With a pressurised water heater the other Grohe requirements must be checked (2 °C, 5 l/⁠min). Write the water heater model in the form.

We leave the private house empty in winter. What must be done with a thermostatic tap?

Grohe states that when the house water supply is drained, the thermostat must be drained separately, because its connections contain non-return valves, and the tap has to be removed from the wall for this.

After the new tap, the pipes knock. Is the tap faulty?

Not necessarily. When a lever tap is closed quickly, the flow stops suddenly and water hammer occurs in the pipes. Close the lever more slowly; if the knock stays, a shock absorber can be fitted.
