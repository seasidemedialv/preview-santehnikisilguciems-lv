Shower cabin installation - tray material and thermostat requirements

Shower cabin installation in Iļģuciems and nearby starts with two questions: what the tray is made of and what kind of mixer the cabin has.

The tray material decides how it is set and sealed. A thermostatic mixer needs sufficiently hot water and a certain flow. Assembly, sealing and connecting to the water and the drain cost from €125 per set; labour only, materials are extra.

What the price from €125 per cabin set covers

The price row applies to a bought cabin where the water supply pipes and the drain are already in place.

  • Included: assembling the cabin and the tray to the manufacturer’s diagram.
  • Included: sealing the joints.
  • Included: connecting to the cold and hot water and the drain.
  • Not included: removing and carrying out the old bath.
  • Not included: the drain pipe up to the riser, from €30 per connection point.
  • Not included: a new water supply pipe to the cabin, from €10/m.
  • Not included: a pressure reducer, if the pressure is too high for the thermostat (you talk through the price on the call).
  • Not included: tiling and an electrical supply for a fan or lighting (an electrician does that).

Tray, drain, check, sealant bead: the order of work

  1. The tray type sets the base For the Ravak Gigant Pro cast-marble tray, the manufacturer gives an installation height of 3 cm and a drain opening of 9 cm. It can be built into the floor with no step, placed on the floor or placed on support feet.
  2. A steel enamel tray with no mortar contact Kaldewei requires that mortar or adhesive does not touch the tray’s lower edge directly, and recommends an installation tape. During installation the enamel must not touch metal.
  3. A drain with a double-lip seal For a steel enamel tray, Kaldewei specifies a drain with a double-lip seal.
  4. Drain check before finishing Once the drain is connected, it is checked before the final finishing, while the trap can still be reached from the side.
  5. Sealant bead with the tray loaded Before the elastic sealant bead, the tray is loaded, and the load stays until the bead has dried. That way the bead forms in the position the tray will have when a person stands in it.
  6. Adjusting the thermostat Grohe thermostatic mixers are set at the factory at a flow pressure of 3 bar on both sides. If the temperature in the shower differs from the scale, the thermostat is adjusted to local conditions.

For a thermostat the hot water must be warmer than the shower

Grohe states three conditions: the hot water at the inlet must be at least 2 °C warmer than the shower water you want, the flow at least 5 l/⁠min, and above 5 bar static pressure a pressure reducer must be fitted. With an open-outlet (pressureless) heater the thermostat does not work, and with a flow-through heater only from 18 kW of power.

In a block of flats with a modernised automated heat substation, the hot water is 50–55 degrees according to Rīgas siltums data. The Iļģuciems and Grīva residential areas are connected to the networks of the “Imanta” and “Zasulauks” heat plants, so on the day of their hydraulic test there is no hot water at any mixer.

What to measure and photograph before the request

  • The cabin manufacturer, the model and the size in centimetres.
  • The tray material: acrylic, steel with enamel or cast marble.
  • The mixer type: thermostatic or single-lever.
  • How the water is heated: the building’s hot water, a water heater or a flow-through heater (with its power in kW).
  • The floor, the lift and whether the bath is still in place.
  • Photos: the whole bathroom, the drain inlet and the supply pipes at the wall.

Shower cabin repair: the shower head drips or it leaks at the base

Drips from a shower are not worth putting off. The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA WaterSense) writes that a shower dripping 10 drops a minute wastes more than 500 US gallons of water a year.

After each service of the thermostat cartridge, Grohe requires the temperature to be set again. For a leaking compression nut, the UK plumbers' register WaterSafe recommends tightening it carefully by a quarter turn while holding the body still. If water comes through the sealant bead at the base, the old bead is cut out and the new one is applied with the tray loaded. A repair is charged at the hourly rate, from €25/h.

A shower in a new place above a neighbour’s room

In series blocks the bathrooms are usually one above another. If the shower is moved to another room, LBN 200-21 requires that the sound insulation, waterproofing, ventilation and other requirements be met above the living rooms of the flat below.

A representative of the Riga Building Board explained in LV portāls (Latvia’s official legal information portal) in 2020: installing a sanitary unit above the living rooms of the flat below needs the consent of the owner of that flat. The work is usually registered with a confirmation card. The article is old, so ask the Building Board. Checked 16.11.2026.

Questions residents ask

Why will the cabin thermostat not turn past 38 °C?

Grohe thermostats have a safety stop at 38 °C. How to override the stop on a particular model is written in the mixer’s manual.

The house has a flow-through heater. Does a cabin with a thermostat work?

Grohe states that a thermostat works with a flow-through heater only if the heater’s power is at least 18 kW. It cannot be used with an open-outlet heater. In the form, write the heater’s model.

Do I need the building manager’s permission to change a cabin?

The Apartment Ownership Law counts the shower equipment and the pipes up to the riser as part of the flat, and work in a flat can be done without agreement with other owners provided common property is untouched. When the riser must be closed, the building manager does that, so ask them in good time.
