In a flat in a series block, the water heater is a back-up for the summer day when the district heating network is tested and there is no hot water. In a private house it is the only source of hot water, so the size of the family and the time the tank needs to heat are what matter.

A flat’s water heater affects the neighbour below, a house’s water heater affects the whole house

In a series block the water heater connection starts after the flat’s valves. Under Article 3 of the Apartment Ownership Law, the utility lines as far as the common-property risers, together with the flat’s utility equipment, are separate property, so the water heater is usually the flat owner’s responsibility, unless the building’s documents say otherwise. Article 14 of the same law says a flat owner is liable for damage caused to others. If the water heater leaks, the water from your floor can reach the neighbour on the floor below.

In a private house the incoming supply pipe, the pipes and the water heater belong to one owner, and no neighbour suffers from a leak. But when the water heater does not heat, there is no hot water at any tap. In the middle of Kleisti, the “Spilves pļavas” private houses grew out of rebuilt allotments: in such a house the water heater runs every day, and its volume should be chosen by the family, not by the free space on the wall.

A water heater for the district heating test day

In summer, AS “Rīgas siltums” (Riga’s district heating company) tests the heating networks zone by zone. The test lasts one day: the evening before, the heat carrier is cooled to +45 degrees, and if no faults are found, hot water is restored that same evening. When the networks of the “Imanta” and “Zasulauks” heat plants are tested, there is also no hot water in the Iļģuciems and Grīva residential areas.

For a back-up water heater the heating time matters. The manufacturer (Gorenje) states that with a 2000 W heating element, water goes from 10 °C to 65 °C in 1 h 38 min in a 50-litre tank, 2 h 37 min in an 80-litre tank and 3 h 16 min in a 100-litre tank. If on test day you switch the water heater on only in the morning, the first shower comes after that time.

Water heater volume for a family in a house

In a private house the family decides the water heater volume: how many people shower one after another in the evening and whether someone fills a bath. You talk through the litres on the call; in the form, write how many people live in the house and whether it has a bath.

A bigger tank takes longer to heat. In Gorenje’s data, a 100-litre tank with the same 2000 W heating element takes twice as long as a 50-litre tank. For a family that washes in the evening, the time from cold to hot matters as much as the litres. For a flat’s back-up on test day, a smaller tank is enough, because it works a few days a year.

If a family fills a bath in the evening, a large share of the tank goes at once, and the next person has to wait for water. A bigger tank also needs a stronger wall and more space above the water heater for the anode replacement; the manufacturers' requirements are on the page water heater installation.

Three things not to do yourself with a water heater

  • Do not leave a full water heater in a house where frost threatens or the power will be off for more than two months. The manufacturer (Atlantic) recommends emptying the water heater in such cases.
  • Do not press the safety thermostat button while the water heater is live. In the manufacturer’s (Electrolux) description, resetting starts with disconnecting from the power and opening the service panel with a tool. If the thermostat cuts out repeatedly, the cause must be found.
  • Do not hang a water heater over a floor with no waterproofing and no drain unless there is a tray under it. The manufacturer (Thermex) requires a protective tray under the water heater with an outlet to the drains in such places.

Installation from €70, descaling from €75, repair after inspection

Hanging a water heater and connecting it to the existing supply pipes with a safety valve is from €70 per unit. Limescale removal with an inspection of the heating element and the anode is from €75. Repair has no single price: it is set by the faulty part, if the water heater has to be drained and removed from the wall, whether spare parts exist for the model and whether the repair costs more than a new water heater. New pipes to the water heater’s position are from €10/m. Labour only; materials are extra.

Questions residents ask

How do I find out which day our building will have no hot water?

“Rīgas siltums” informs building managers about tests and interruptions. Ask your building manager or look for a notice in the stairwell. Then you can switch on the back-up water heater in good time.

On test day, should the back-up water heater be switched on in the morning or already in the evening?

In the evening. The heat carrier is cooled to +45 degrees the evening before, so the building’s water turns cooler before the test itself. The manufacturer (Gorenje) gives 2 h 37 min from 10 °C to 65 °C for an 80-litre tank with a 2000 W heating element, so a water heater switched on in the evening usually has heated by morning.

Do I need the neighbours' or the building manager’s consent to fit a water heater in a flat?

Article 11 of the Apartment Ownership Law lets an owner carry out work inside their flat without agreement with other owners, as long as it does not affect common property or other flats and complies with regulations. A water heater after the flat’s valves usually does not affect the riser. If the riser has to be touched, ask your building manager.

What should I do with the water heater if nobody will be at the summer cottage or house for a month?

The manufacturer (Gorenje) describes a frost-protection position in which the water heater holds about 10 °C and the power need not be disconnected. If you disconnect the power for more than two months or frost threatens the room, Atlantic recommends emptying the water heater.

What should I say about the water heater on the call?

The volume in litres, whether it is in a flat or a private house, what is happening (not heating, leaking, smelling) and how many years it has been on the wall. A photo of the water heater’s label helps to find the model.
