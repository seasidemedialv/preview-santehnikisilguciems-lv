The Iļģuciems prison and health centre are in Dzirciems

Until the 1970s the present Dzirciems was called Iļģuciems, and only after that were the Nordeķi and Zasulauks territories next to Dzirciema iela called Dzirciems. The old name stayed with places: according to OpenStreetMap, the Iļģuciems women’s prison on Tvaikoņu iela and the Riga Health Centre branch “Iļģuciems” at Sēlpils iela 15 are in the Dzirciems neighbourhood (our map reading).

For plumbing this changes just one thing: in the form field “District” choose the one that matches the street, and in the address field write the street, building number and floor. If in doubt, choose either of the two. The plumber goes to the address, not to the neighbourhood name.

Twelve floors on one riser

In a series 104 block the water and soil risers run from the cellar to the top floor through each flat’s toilet duct. A section can be replaced in a single flat, but the building manager shuts off the water riser along its whole height. So twelve flats are without water for that time, and the working time is chosen together with the building manager.

The new section must be joined to the neighbouring sections above and below you. If they are old, the transition from the new material to the old pipe is made right at the ceiling and the floor. In a five-storey series 464 block the principle is the same, but the shut-off affects fewer flats.

Apartment Ownership Law, Article 13: the flat owner pays for replacing communications in common property, for example shared risers, according to their share, following a decision of the owners' association. Who pays for replacing one section is usually set by the building’s decisions; ask your building manager. Checked 16.11.2026.

Hot water comes from the Imanta and Zasulauks networks

When AS “Rīgas siltums” tests the heating networks of the heat plants “Imanta” and “Zasulauks”, there is no hot water in the residential districts of Dzirciems and Dzegužkalns either. The tests are carried out in summer by zone and last one day, and the hot water is restored that evening if no damage is found. The heat supplier informs building managers about the interruption.

A one-day interruption is not a problem for many people. If the flat has a small child or someone ill, or if hot water in the kitchen runs slowly even every day, a water heater is a reserve. In a series flat it is usually connected to the cold water supply with a safety valve, and the drain from the valve needs a place by the sink or in the toilet duct.

Dzirciema iela drains and the building’s horizontal pipe

Rīgas ūdens (Riga’s water utility) renewed sewer networks by the trenchless method on several streets, including Dzirciema iela. That is the city network in the street. The building’s horizontal pipe in the cellar and the risers are usually the building’s common property, while the flat’s drain pipes up to the riser are usually the flat owner’s responsibility. Under Article 9 of the Residential Building Management Law, the building owner is responsible for managing the building, and in a multi-flat building it is made up of the flat owners.

In practice: if your sink or bath is blocked, it is usually the flat’s part of the drain up to the riser. If water climbs into the lower flat’s bath or toilet, the blockage is usually in the riser or the horizontal pipe, and the first call is to the building manager. Renovating the building is also a decision of the building: the list of buildings renovated by Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga’s municipal building manager) includes, for example, a multi-flat building at Dzegužu iela 3 (listed as 2023).

Questions residents ask

I live near Tvaikoņu iela. Which district should I choose in the form, Dzirciems or Iļģuciems?

Our map reading: on OpenStreetMap the Iļģuciems women’s prison on Tvaikoņu iela is in Dzirciems. For a request the neighbourhood is not decisive: the street, the building number and the floor in the address field matter.

A neighbour on the upper floor replaced their riser section. Do I have to replace mine too?

Usually not, unless the owners' association’s decision says otherwise; ask your building manager. Technically the neighbour’s new section joins your old one with a transition fitting. If your section is rusted or leaks at the joints, it is better to replace it while the riser is shut off anyway. Who pays is usually set by the owners' association’s decisions.

Do I need a water heater if the hot water disappears only on the test day?

Not always. For one day, heated water is enough. A water heater is worth it if the flat has a small child, if the hot water in the kitchen has to be run for a long time, or if someone in the family cannot wait until the evening. Installation is from €70, the water heater itself is extra.
