Small-family project buildings the flat’s taps, traps and drain pipes the shared risers and the cellar horizontal pipes

The line between the two columns is not only technical. The Apartment Ownership Law obliges the owner to treat common property with care, and the flat owners together are responsible for managing the building. What you can change yourself and what needs the building manager’s knowledge is decided by exactly this line. If you know your building’s series or year, write it in the form: it helps to understand which pipes are waiting in the wall.

City drains, building drains, flat drains

In its 2024 analysis, Rīgas ūdens (Riga’s water utility) named Āgenskalns among the neighbourhoods with the most sewer network failures in previous years. These are the city networks under the streets, and under the Water Management Services Law the service provider maintains them up to the ownership boundary.

For a flat this leads to a simple question of order: at which section does the water stop? There are three answers.

  1. Only one fixture drains slowly Only the sink or the bath runs slowly, everything else is fine. The blockage is in your branch or trap, and that is the flat’s job.
  2. Water rises in several fixtures or on a lower floor If, when you use water, it shows up in the bath or toilet below, the shared riser or the cellar horizontal pipe is blocked. Report it to the building manager.
  3. Water stands in the yard chamber for the whole building That is the building’s outlet or the city network beyond the ownership boundary. The building manager finds out on which side the fault is.

A flood report when water has reached a neighbour

Our map reading: the Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga’s municipal building manager) customer service centre at Mārtiņa iela 7 is in Āgenskalns, in Āgenskalna priedes, on OpenStreetMap. If Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks manages the building, you request the flood report from the building manager.

Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks issues a flat flood report only to the flat owner or an authorised person, if the request is submitted no later than six months after the flooding. The report is usually prepared within 20 working days. The flat owner is liable for damage to neighbours as set out in legislation, in the decisions of the owners' association or in contracts (Apartment Ownership Law, Article 14).

While the report is being prepared, the leak must be stopped. Close the flat’s valve by the meter (usually it is before the meter, sometimes after it), photograph the spot where it leaks and keep the parts you replaced. If it leaks from the riser, only the building manager shuts it.

Where to get drinking water while the riser is shut off

Rīgas ūdens public drinking taps in Āgenskalns are on the forecourt of Āgenskalna tirgus at Nometņu iela 64, by Māras dīķis and in the renovated Uzvaras parks. For a riser section replacement the water is shut off for the whole riser, so for all flats on it, and during the work the kitchen tap is dry for the neighbours too.

So prepare water in advance. In season you can take water for tea and food from a public tap, and for flushing the toilet a bucket filled before the shut-off is enough.

Questions residents ask

Who pays for replacing the shared riser in a sand-lime brick block?

Apartment Ownership Law, Article 13: the flat owner pays for replacing communications in common property according to their share, following a decision of the owners' association. Usually it works this way, unless the building’s documents say otherwise; ask your building manager.

How long after a flood can I ask for a flood report?

In buildings managed by Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks the request is submitted no later than six months after the flooding, and the report is usually prepared within 20 working days. With another building manager the procedure can differ.

Can the riser be replaced only on my own floor?

A section within one flat can be replaced, with transition fittings to the neighbouring sections. This needs the building manager’s permission and the water shut off for the whole riser. If the sections above and below are in poor condition, it is better for the building to decide on the whole riser.
