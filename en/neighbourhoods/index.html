Where the series blocks stand

Imanta is dominated by five-storey series 464 and 467 blocks and nine-storey series 602 panel blocks. Zolitūde was started in 1983, and series 119 buildings with sections of 6–10 storeys and 16-storey towers dominate there. Dzirciems has series 464 five-storey blocks and series 104 12-storey blocks, Bolderāja has series 464 and 103, and Daugavgrīva mainly series 103 blocks.

In these blocks the water and soil risers are common property, heating and hot water usually come from Rīgas siltums networks, and work on a riser or a radiator needs the building manager to be told. The taller the block, the more flats there are on one riser.

Hot water in Iļģuciems, Dzirciems, Zasulauks, Šampēteris and Zolitūde comes from the Imanta and Zasulauks heating plant networks, so on the day of these networks' summer test it disappears in all these neighbourhoods at once. Bolderāja is supplied by the Rīgas siltums boiler house at Gobas iela 33a.

Where people live in private houses

In the middle part of Kleisti is the private house estate “Spilves pļavas”, which grew out of rebuilt allotments. Spilve and Voleri are private-house neighbourhoods with very few residents: in 2025, 69 people lived in Spilve and 211 in Voleri. In Imanta, detached-house areas take up 14% of the neighbourhood, for example around Bebru iela. In Šampēteris, Rīgas ūdens built new sewer networks over the last 15 years; that is the street network, but the line in the yard is the owner’s responsibility.

In a private house the owner looks after the heating boiler’s circuit, the water heater, the yard sewer line and the incoming supply pipe, so jobs here are more often outside the house and at the heat source itself.

Ķīpsala and Āgenskalns are a separate story

Ķīpsala has the RTU student campus built in the 1970s, and old courtyards with surviving outbuildings. Āgenskalns has series 316/318 silicate-brick blocks and five-storey brick blocks. Descriptions of each place are on the neighbourhood pages below.

The trip to the farther neighbourhoods
