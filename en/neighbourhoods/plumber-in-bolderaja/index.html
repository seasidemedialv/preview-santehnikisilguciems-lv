Risers replaced at Keramikas iela 2 in 2019

In 2019 Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga’s municipal building manager) replaced the water supply and soil risers in the building at Keramikas iela 2 in Bolderāja. It shows how riser questions are usually handled in such buildings: the whole riser is replaced by the building together, not flat by flat on each floor.

Article 13 of the Apartment Ownership Law says that the flat owner pays for replacing communications in common property, for example shared risers, according to their share of the common property, following a decision of the flat owners' association. What exactly is common property in your building, ask your building manager.

If the building has not yet replaced its risers but a section on your floor leaks, one section can be replaced. This needs the building manager’s permission and the water shut off for the whole riser, so the neighbours above and below are without water during the work. After the building’s risers are replaced, the flat may also need its supply pipes from the new riser to the taps and the toilet redone.

Where does your part end? In a multi-flat building the Water Management Services Law draws a line between the Rīgas ūdens network and the building’s system, which is common property of the flat owners. Inside the building the risers belong to this shared system, while the pipes after the flat’s valves usually belong to the flat owner, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

Apartment Ownership Law, Article 13 (costs of common property); Water Management Services Law (ownership boundary). Checked 16.11.2026.

The Gobas iela boiler house and a day without hot water

Bolderāja’s hot water and heating network is supplied by the AS “Rīgas siltums” boiler house at Gobas iela 33a. When Rīgas siltums tests the heating networks in summer, there is no hot water in Bolderāja on the test day.

The tests are carried out by zone and last one day. The evening before, the heat carrier is cooled to +45 degrees, and the hot water is restored that evening if no damage is found. Rīgas siltums informs building managers about the interruptions, so ask your building manager for the date.

If a one-day interruption is too long for a family, a small water heater can be installed in the kitchen or bathroom. It is connected with a safety valve on the cold water supply, and no shut-off valves may be placed between the water heater and this valve (manufacturer Dražice). What to check when there is no hot water is in the advice on no hot water in the flat.

Questions residents ask

The building will replace the risers for the whole building. What must be prepared in the flat?

Usually access to the riser in the toilet duct or behind a panel is needed, so the duct must be opened. What access is needed and who closes the duct afterwards, ask your building manager or the contractor before the work.

Can I replace only my own riser section in a series 464 block?

Yes, if the building manager allows it and shuts off the riser. The new section is joined to the neighbouring sections with transition fittings. Labour is from €110 for a water section and from €100 for a soil stack section on one floor.

When I wash dishes, water rises in the bath or toilet. Where is the blockage?

If water from the kitchen sink shows up in the bath, the blockage is usually in the flat’s drain before the riser, and it is cleared with a drain cable (from €50). If waste water rises in a lower-floor flat from other flats' water, the riser is blocked, and that must be reported to the building manager.

How do I find out on which day there will be no hot water in Bolderāja?

Rīgas siltums informs building managers about heating network tests. Ask your building manager when the test is planned for your building’s zone.
